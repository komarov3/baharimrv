MODULE 0

Shared Identity & Onboarding — "Blue ID"

§5.3 requires a common user identity system across all three modules. Every actor onboards once, receives a blockchain-anchored decentralised identifier (DID) and a custodial wallet, then sees only the modules matching their role. Designed for low digital literacy: icon-first, voice-assisted, Kiswahili/English.
