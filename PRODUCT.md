## Users

Community project managers and field monitors working in coastal field conditions, independent verifiers, carbon buyers, finance operators, administrators, and public proof verifiers. Jahazi and BahariMRV users also need secure bidirectional sign-in without sharing passwords.

## Product Purpose

BahariMRV is the fourth BahariLink platform and its conservation module. It manages conservation projects, offline monitoring, independent verification, IOTA testnet provenance, on-demand proof documents, and registry-backed blue-carbon workflows.

## Positioning

BahariMRV connects field evidence to independently verifiable conservation outcomes. It distinguishes manager-attested completion from verified achievement and generates proofs that check real IOTA state rather than treating a database hash as verification.

## Operating Context

Field monitors capture GPS, survival counts, species, photos, and voice observations offline. Project managers track plots, wallets, alerts, and reports. Verifiers review field and satellite evidence. Buyers use registry-backed marketplace inventory. Every registered user receives an IOTA testnet identity.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Native Android field app and responsive React/TypeScript web portal.
- Go/Gin/GORM/PostgreSQL backend and IOTA Rebased testnet adapters.
- Authorization-code SSO with PKCE between Jahazi and BahariMRV through a shared BahariLink identity contract and SDK.
- Managed KMS-backed key recovery, with no plaintext fallback.
- Provider-neutral satellite, registry, and payment sandboxes until providers are selected.
- Read-only BahariLink financial imports for the first release.
- No fabricated verification, registry, payment, GPS, or impact states.

## Brand Commitments

Use the BahariMRV name within the BahariLink product family. Preserve the supplied conservation wireframe's clean, compact, mobile-first operations experience and its Home, Monitor, Credits, Wallet, and Profile navigation.
